## Ranked proposals

### 1. `spreadpess=0.25` (new key)

- **Mechanism:** When worlds disagree on a candidate’s value, raise pessimism before the mean/worst blend — targets winner’s curse without a flat `pess` on every move.
- **Evidence:** Median world spread 8–22 leaf points on review moments; margins above runner-up often &lt;10 while spreads are wider.
- **Expected effect:** Small positive Elo on noisy lines; neutral on forced tactics.
- **Time cost:** Zero (aggregation only).
- **Sweep candidate:** `h0:nodes=32000,horizon=3,k=8,tkill=10000,tkroll=8,hbcheck=2000,fuseguard=1,okill=15,hbk=16,alloc=world,threads=1,lostrank=4000,order=h0-order-v59,oorder=h0-order-v53,oopen=1,onext=0,deal=block,net=results/run87/leaf87q.json,mull=leaf,mulls=256,spreadpess=0.25`

### 2. `wargmax=1` (new key)

- **Mechanism:** Majority vote across worlds for the root action; breaks winner’s curse when one world overestimates a line.
- **Evidence:** Worlds disagree on the argmax candidate on roughly one third of review decisions with `k≥8`.
- **Expected effect:** Modest Elo if curse dominates; risk on single-world lethal consensus.
- **Time cost:** Zero.
- **Sweep candidate:** same baseline with `wargmax=1`.

### 3. `pess=0.25` (existing)

- **Mechanism:** Static blend toward worst world (only sweep 7 on a pre-network bot).
- **Evidence:** High world spread; sweep 7 suggested signal before `onext=0` stack.
- **Expected effect:** +2–6 Elo if curse persists; may hurt lines where one bad world is noise.
- **Time cost:** Zero.
- **Sweep candidate:** baseline with `pess=0.25`.

### 4. `hres=400` with `horizon=3` (existing)

- **Mechanism:** More reserve nodes for finish-and-reply without raising search depth.
- **Evidence:** `horizon_nodes` often bumps `hres` cap on cut-off lines; default `hres=200`.
- **Expected effect:** +2–5 Elo on horizon-fallback lines; +5–10% wall time.
- **Time cost:** modest (charged horizon work only).
- **Sweep candidate:** baseline with `hres=400` (depth capped at `horizon=3`).

### 5. `k=16` at sweep scale (existing)

- **Mechanism:** More worlds at same `nodes` cap → less nodes/world but more vote diversity.
- **Evidence:** Served uses `k=16`; sweep scale uses `k=8`; disagreement rate drops with low `k` on several portals lines.
- **Expected effect:** Trade speed vs stability; worth re-measuring after leaf87q.
- **Time cost:** Near-neutral wall time (`alloc=world`).
- **Sweep candidate:** baseline with `k=16` (same `nodes=32000`).
