# H0 search speed — November 2026

## Machine

- **CPU:** Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor (`nproc=4`)
- **Build:** `cargo test --release -p arena-engine`
- **Baseline parent commit:** `edde4b5`

## Problem

Decision time is dominated by the **opponent model** (`oopen`, `opp_lethal`), then
**order networks** (~1.3M calls) and **leaf encodes**; the value net **forward pass**
is only ~1.5% of wall time. Bit-identical caching cannot recover much of the budget.

## Duplicate work within one decision (`h0-prof`)

| Kind | sweep repeat rate | served repeat rate |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|
| leaf_value / leaf_encode | 37.0% | 37.6% |
| opp_lethal | 29.9% | 30.1% |
| oopen | 29.3% | 29.7% |

Probe keys: `search_key_id(state)` XOR seat (state identity across worlds).

**Why bit-identical cache does not help much:** repeats are common, but **leaf_forward is ~1.5%** of wall time; even a perfect leaf cache saves at most ~0.5% overall (37% × 1.5%). Encode repeats (~10% zone) would need a separate bit-identical encode cache; **opp_lethal** / **oopen** need `line`, node cap, and PV accounting — not safe on state-only keys.

A **per-decision leaf net cache** (TLS bind + shared `Mutex`, wasm workers) was prototyped on this branch, measured **sub-1%** on the harness, and **removed** (complexity not justified).

## Bit-identical changes shipped

### `apply_known_legal` on proven-legal hot paths

Skip redundant `legal_actions` where the action is already from a legal list.

**Measured gain:** ~**2–4%** on the 52-case harness vs `edde4b5`; review 12-pack sweep ~390.6 → ~387 ms.

## Remaining levers (behaviour-changing; not implemented)

Estimates are **fraction of decision wall time** saved if the policy/spec were relaxed (sweep/served scale, `threads=1`). They **change decisions** and are not pinned today.

| Lever | Est. wall share today | Change (examples) | Est. time saved |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **`oopen`** | 30–38% | Lower `oopen` depth, tighter reply beam, or cap reply nodes under budget pressure | **15–25%** |
| **`opp_lethal`** | 21–22% | Disable `olethal` in fast modes, lower `okill` apply caps, or skip lethal re-check when face unchanged | **10–18%** |
| **`order_net`** | 15–16% | Drop `order=` / `oorder=` on opponent paths, or rank with value net only | **8–14%** |
| **`leaf_encode`** (order + value) | ~10% | Fewer expanded nodes (deeper cuts), smaller beam, or cheaper encoding — not a forward-pass win | **3–7%** |

Parallel search (`threads>1` on served) can add **near-linear** speedup per decision but changes job scheduling under explain and is a deployment choice, not a default pin change.
