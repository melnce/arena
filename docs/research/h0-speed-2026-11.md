## Problem

Decision time is dominated by the **opponent model** (`oopen`, `opp_lethal`), then
**order networks** (~1.3M calls) and **leaf encodes**; the value net **forward pass**
is only ~1.5% of wall time. Bit-identical caching cannot recover much of the budget.

## `h0-prof` attribution (feature `h0-prof`, 52 cases, wall shares)

Sweep: **`oopen` ~37%**, **`opp_lethal` ~21%**, **`order_net` ~16%**,
**`leaf_encode` ~10%**, **`leaf_forward` ~1.5%** (nested inside own search).

Served: **`oopen` ~38%**, **`opp_lethal` ~22%**, **`order_net` ~16%**.

Duplicate rates (~37% leaf encode, ~30% opp lethal / `oopen`) are measured only
with `--features h0-prof`; default builds call `prof::dup_probe(kind, || key)` and
the stub never runs the closure (no `search_key_id` work).


## Bit-identical change shipped: `apply_known_legal`

Skip redundant `legal_actions` where the action is already from a legal list.

**World roots:** candidates come from `legal_actions(db, decision_state)`. Each
root is `determinize_block(decision_state, me, …)`; for served-style specs
(`info=open`/`draws`, `deal=block`) only opponent hidden cards and the
perspective deck **order** change (`resample_own_deck` does not touch the hand).
Own-turn legality for bot candidates does not depend on opponent hidden cards, so
each candidate stays legal on every root; `apply_known_legal` matches `apply` when
legal (same as on the decision state).

**Measured gain:** interleaved harness vs `edde4b5` (final head); previously
~2–4% on non-interleaved 52-case runs, ~1% on served at 93-decision scale.

**Removed:** per-decision leaf net cache (sub-1%, TLS + `Mutex` complexity).
